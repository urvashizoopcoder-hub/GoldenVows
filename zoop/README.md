## ✨ What Is Included

### 1. Interactive Incoming Wedding Call
- Realistic smartphone call screen simulation with clock `9:41`, cellular/WiFi/battery status.
- Couple artwork with vibration and ringing sound effects.
- **"Slide to answer"** slider that unlocks the full invitation upon answering.

### 2. Ambient Animations & Effects
- **Floating Paper Lanterns**: Rising into the royal night sky.
- **HTML5 Falling Petals Canvas**: Fluttering rose and marigold petals drifting gently across the screen.
- **Interactive Save The Date Envelope**: Tap the envelope to pull out the calendar and see the wedding date highlighted.
- **Acoustic Background Music**: Authentic piano audio (`assets/music.mp3`).

### 3. Live Customizer (`edit.html`)
- **💑 The Couple**: Groom & Bride names, parents' blessing text, photo upload.
- **📅 Wedding Date & Venue**: Date, time, venue address, and auto-generated Google Maps link.
- **🪔 Events & Functions**: Quick-add presets (Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception, Cocktail) with custom dates, venues, notes, and dress codes.
- **💌 Messages & Quotes**: Invitation text, sacred vow quote, closing quote, and hashtag.
- **✅ RSVP & Audio Settings**: WhatsApp RSVP number (direct WhatsApp chat link generator), reply deadline, music toggle, and photo gallery toggle.
- **Instant Shareable Link Generator**: Compresses your custom configuration directly into the URL hash (`#data=...`).
